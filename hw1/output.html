Finals schedule

A two-hour time period is allotted for each final class meeting. All classes are required to meet during the final week of instruction at the appropriate time and dates indicated below.

  • Four credit, MTWR classes will hold final exams based on the MW regular class occurrence.
  • If you have an irresolvable conflict during the final week of classes, contact your instructor to arrange an alternative.

Wednesday, Prep Day

No classes held. Prepare for final week of instruction and testing.
